Best apps to quit porn and lust

Nine iPhone apps for breaking a porn or lust habit, from big communities to a private blocker. Full disclosure: we make Lustless, one of the apps below. So every fact here comes from each app's own App Store page, checked on 21 October 2026, and we say where another app is the better pick.

The quick answer.

  • A big community and lessons: QUITTR or Brainbuddy.
  • Private, with a blocker and nothing collected: Lustless (ours).
  • A faith-based approach: Unchaind or Overcomer.
  • Live group support: Relay.
  • Just a free day counter: I Am Sober.
Whatever you pick: one slip doesn't erase the days before it. Note what triggered it, fix that one thing, and start again the same day.

Fair questions.

What is the best app to quit porn?

It depends on what helps you. For a large community and lessons, QUITTR and Brainbuddy are the best known. For a faith-based approach, Unchaind and Overcomer. For live group support, Relay. For a private counter, panic button and blocker with no account, Lustless.

Is there a free app to quit porn?

Several have a free part. Lustless's counter, calendar, panic button, urge log and daily check-in are free with no time limit. I Am Sober is a free day counter for any habit. Most of the others put their main features behind a subscription.

Can an app block porn on iPhone?

Yes. iPhone apps use Apple's Screen Time controls to turn on Apple's own adult-content filter and block chosen sites and apps, in Safari and other browsers. Lustless, Overcomer and BlockerX all describe blocking. Any blocker can be switched off by the person holding the phone: it is a guard rail, not a lock.

Is this medical advice?

No. These apps are tools, not treatment. If this habit is affecting your health, your work or your relationships, a doctor or a licensed therapist can help in ways no app can.
