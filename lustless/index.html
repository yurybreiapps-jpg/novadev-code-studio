What it does

The NSFW Shield

Apple's own adult-content filter is on whenever the Shield is on, plus any sites you add and any apps or categories you pick. It holds in Safari and in other browsers.

Every clean day, counted

A live counter to the second, a calendar of clean days, and which phase you're in with a note on what it tends to feel like.

A panic button with your own reasons

The reasons you wrote down on day one, then four rounds of guided breathing, then a one-tap note of how it went.

Log urges. See what sets them off.

A one-tap urge and relapse log, and patterns drawn from your own entries. Never a made-up “recovery score”.

A 90-day guide, phase by phase

What each phase tends to feel like and what tends to help. A guide, not medical advice.

On iPad too

The same app, laid out for the bigger screen — one purchase covers iPhone and iPad on the same Apple ID.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in

Nothing you write leaves your device. There is no server on our side to send it to.

A Face ID lock for the app

Turn it on in Settings and the app asks for Face ID every time it opens.

The Shield can't see what you blocked

iOS hands the app sealed references it can block but never read. The names and icons in your list are drawn by iOS itself.

Free and Pro

Free, forever: the counter and calendar, the panic button and breathing, your reasons, the urge and relapse log, the daily check-in.

Pro: the Shield, the full 90-day guide, and your patterns. Monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial; cancel any time in your Apple ID settings. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store.
