Help with AI Image Generator Anywhere.

Written by the person who made it.

Which iPhones and iPads can run it?

The app needs a device with at least 6 GB of memory. That is the single most common reason it will not work, so it is worth checking first.

It runs on: iPhone 12 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 13 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 14 Pro and Pro Max, iPhone 15 and later of every kind, iPhone 16 and 17 of every kind, iPad Pro and iPad Air with an M-series chip, and later iPads.

It cannot run on: iPhone 11 and earlier, iPhone 12 and 12 mini, iPhone 13 and 13 mini, iPhone 14 and 14 Plus, any iPhone SE, and older iPads. These have 4 GB of memory or less. The image model cannot even finish preparing itself on them — this is a hard limit of the hardware, not something an update can fix.

If you bought the app and your device turns out to be one of these, ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com and let us know, so we can improve the warning.

Why is there a 3 GB download?

Because the thing that draws your images is on your device, not on a server. That is the whole point of the app, and a model of that size is what it takes to make good pictures.

It downloads once, over Wi-Fi, and is then yours. You can pause it and come back to it. After that the app never needs the internet again.

"Preparing the model for this device" is taking minutes

That is expected the first time, and it happens once. Your device is compiling the model to run on its Neural Engine, which is what makes every image after that take seconds instead of minutes.

It happens again after an app update, because iOS discards the compiled copy when the app changes. Leave the app open and it will finish.

My edit changed the wrong thing

This is almost always the protection setting, and it is the one thing worth understanding.

A change is never confined to the thing you named — it lands on whatever the app is allowed to touch. So if you protect the wrong part, the change goes somewhere else instead:

  • Changing clothes, hair or a person's appearance? Choose Keep background. With Keep subject the clothing is protected too, so "make my shirt red" leaves the shirt alone and reddens the room.
  • Changing the scene behind someone? Use the New background job with Keep subject. Do not use Edit for this — the editor repaints what is already in the picture rather than building a new scene, so asking it for a forest tints everything green.
  • Restyling the whole picture? Use Restyle, and turn on Keep faces recognisable if you want the people to still look like themselves.

The app watches for this and will warn you before it starts, with a one-tap fix. If you see that warning, take it.

Hands and faces came out wrong

Hands are the hardest thing for any image model, this one included, and the app will sometimes get them wrong. It is a limit of the technology rather than a setting you have missed.

Two things genuinely help. Press Regenerate — a fresh attempt at the same words is the most reliable fix, and often the very next one is clean. And make three or five at once and keep the best, which is the same idea with less waiting.

Prompts that ask for hands doing something specific — holding, gripping, pointing — are the hardest of all. "With flowers" tends to work far better than "holding flowers".

Where are my images, and can I sell them?

They are in the app's Gallery, on your device. Tap one to see it full size, swipe to move through them, and use Save to put a copy in your photo library or Share to send it anywhere.

They are yours. We claim no rights over anything you make, and you are free to use your images commercially.

Because they live on your device rather than in an account, they are not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes them. Save anything you want to keep to your photo library.

How do I free up space?

In Settings, swipe left on any model to remove it. That frees its full size immediately, and you can download it again whenever you like. Removing the Photo Editor keeps image generation working; removing the Photoreal model returns the app to its setup screen.

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. Once the model has downloaded, turn on Airplane Mode and everything still works — generating, editing, the gallery, all of it. That is not a side effect; it is what the app is for.

Still stuck?

Write to us. A real person reads it, and a note saying which device you have and what you were trying to do gets you a faster answer.
