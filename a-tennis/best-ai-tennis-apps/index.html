Best AI tennis apps to analyze your swing

Eight iPhone apps that use AI to look at your tennis, from full match tracking to a score for every stroke. Full disclosure: we make AI Tennis Swing Coach, one of the apps below. So every fact here comes from each app's own App Store page, checked on 21 October 2026, and we say where another app is the better pick.

The quick answer.

  • Match stats, line calls and shot speed: SwingVision.
  • A score and one fix for every swing, free on your iPhone: AI Tennis Swing Coach (ours).
  • Swing analysis without a subscription: sevensix, from $1.99 an analysis.
  • Film a whole match from the fence: Toach.
  • Swing tracking from an Apple Watch: Tennis Plus.
Whatever you pick: film from the side with your whole body and racket in frame. A steady phone and good light do more for the analysis than any setting.

The best known

1. SwingVision: Tennis Pickleball

Best for: match stats, line calls and shot speed.

The most established app here: automated stats on shot speed, depth and accuracy, highlights and line calls from a single camera, with an Apple Watch companion. It is the official tracking app of Tennis Australia, the LTA and the ITA. If you want to analyze matches rather than single strokes, start here.

  • Listed in-app purchases: Plus $14.99 a month or $95.99 a year; Pro $24.99 or $179.99; Max $39.99 or $299.99
  • Ratings: 4,957 (4.7)
  • App Privacy: declares Data Used to Track You
  • iPad: yes; needs iOS 18

5. TorqAI: Tennis Swing Doctor

Best for: seeing your swing as a 3D body next to a pro's.

Tracks 17 body joints frame by frame and rebuilds the swing in 3D, with a similarity score against a chosen pro.

Fair questions.

Can an iPhone analyze my tennis swing without sensors?

Yes. Every app on this page works from video shot on the phone's camera, with no sensors on the racket. AI Tennis Swing Coach's free Visual Coach tracks your body and racket on the iPhone itself, live or from a recorded clip. The one exception here is Tennis Plus, which reads your swing from an Apple Watch.

Is there a free AI tennis coach app?

Several have a free part. AI Tennis Swing Coach's Visual Coach, with a score for every swing, is free with no limit and no account. Toach's free plan analyzes the first 2 minutes of each match. sevensix sells single analyses from $1.99 if you would rather not subscribe.

How should I film my tennis swing for AI analysis?

Film from the side so your whole body and racket stay in frame, keep the phone still at about hip height, and use 60 frames per second if your phone offers it. Good light matters more than resolution.

Which app is best for match stats and line calls?

SwingVision. It is the most established app here, tracks shot speed, depth and accuracy from one camera, and calls lines. The others focus on your technique rather than the match.
