02

Film your practice.

How to film your tennis swing for analysis — camera angle, frame rate, clip length, and lighting.

  • Side view is best — put your phone on a tripod or fence mount at the side of the court, or have a friend film you. Side view shows contact point, racket path, and follow-through most clearly. Behind the baseline also works for court position and recovery.
  • Full body in frame — shoes, shoulders, racket, ball contact, and the finish should all stay visible. Don't zoom.
  • 30–60 seconds of swings is plenty. Film longer if you like — you'll trim to the best part in the app.
  • Use 60fps if your camera offers it — fast serves and rallies keep twice the detail at contact. (Slow-motion modes aren't needed.)
  • Good light helps the AI see timing, balance, and contact.
Creator tip: film with your normal Camera app and import into AI Tennis Swing Coach — you keep clean raw footage for editing, and you can screen-record the app's analysis for the reveal moment.
03

Visual Coach — instant feedback.

Real-time swing tracking on court: live skeleton, racket speed, and instant spoken coaching cues.

Open the Visual Coach tab. It's 100% free, unlimited, fully on-device, and needs no account. Two ways to use it:

  • Live: point the phone at the court while you play. A live AI skeleton tracks your body and racket, every swing gets a 0–100 score, and the coach speaks instant cues out loud. Tap REC to record the session with the overlays baked in.
  • Replay: load the video you filmed. You get the skeleton frame by frame, a score for every swing, red fault cues at the moment they happen, racket and ball trails, slow-motion, and frame-step buttons.

The Trails button controls the racket swing trail and the ball comet; Loop replays one swing on repeat; the swing / frame buttons jump between detected swings and single frames.

After a replay, a Practice Report sums up the session — keep it to History or discard it. You can also tune how the coach talks to you (detail level, encouraging vs. direct tone, and focus areas like technique, power, spin, or footwork) right on the Visual Coach tab, and open the AI Coaching summary for a written breakdown of what's working and your top priorities.

04

Deep AI analysis.

How AI tennis swing analysis works — 0–100 scores, NTRP-style level estimate, visual checkpoints, and your Fix of the Day.

  1. Open the Analyze tab and import your video (or record right there).
  2. Trim to the cleanest 20–90 seconds — the best swings, not the whole session.
  3. Pick the stroke — Forehand, Backhand, Serve, Volley, and more — or use Auto Detect for mixed play.
  4. Choose the analysis depth and run it.

06

If something's not working.

Common problems filming and analyzing a tennis swing — and how to fix them.

  • "It won't analyze my strokes" — check the four usual causes: the player isn't fully in frame · the video is zoomed in too far · the clip wasn't trimmed to actual swings · you're not signed in (the AI analysis needs the one-tap Sign in with Apple).
  • No swing trail showing — tap Trails in Visual Coach and check the Swing toggle; the trail appears only on real strikes, and only when the player is large enough in frame to track reliably.
  • Scores feel off on fast serves — film at 60fps and from the side; motion blur at 30fps hides the contact moment.
  • Multiple people in frame — the AI follows the most prominent player; film solo drills for the cleanest results.
