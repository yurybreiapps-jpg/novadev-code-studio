First Swing Guide

Record a cleaner clip before the first analysis.

Clean video gives the AI better evidence and makes progress easier to compare later. Use this quick checklist before your first upload.

  1. Record from the side. Side view is best for contact point, racket path, preparation, balance, and follow-through.
  2. Keep the full player visible. Shoes, shoulders, racket, ball contact, and finish should stay inside the frame.
  3. Trim the best segment. Select the clearest part of the clip before running analysis.
  4. Choose the stroke or Auto Detect. Pick forehand, backhand, serve, volley, or use Auto Detect for mixed clips.
  5. Practice one Fix of the Day. Focus on one correction, then upload another clip from the same angle later.
For the court

Useful for lessons, league play, and solo practice.

The app is designed to support coaching, not replace it. Use it when you want a second look at your swing and a more focused plan for what to work on next.

Stroke coverage

  • Forehand, backhand, serve, volley, overhead, return, rally, and footwork review.
  • Auto Detect helps when a clip includes mixed tennis action.

Player progress

  • Save reports, track trends, and organize feedback by player profile.
  • Keep your best practice notes close before the next court session.

Clear boundaries

  • AI feedback is coaching guidance, not medical advice or a certified rating.
  • Better video angle and lighting usually produce more useful results.
AI, privacy, and credits

Clear details before you upload.

A-Tennis is built around short analysis clips, local history, optional Pro access, and transparent AI guidance boundaries.

AI engine

Elite Coach Pro analysis uses the premium Gemini Pro model to review selected video frames, stroke context, timing, player level, and technique details.

What is sent

For AI analysis, the app sends selected key frames from your chosen clip range, clip timing, technique, player level, and analysis mode to the A-Tennis backend for AI review.

What stays local

The original imported video, local trimmed clip, player profiles, saved history, and generated reports stay on your device unless you share or export them.

Credits

  • Free accounts include limited AI credits.
  • A-Tennis Pro includes 100 monthly credits.
  • Quick Coach, Deep Review, and Elite Coach use different credit amounts.

AI feedback is coaching guidance only. It may be inaccurate or incomplete, especially with poor camera angle, low light, partial body visibility, or unclear contact. It is not medical advice, injury advice, a certified rating, or a guaranteed performance result.
