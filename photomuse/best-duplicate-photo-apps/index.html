Best apps to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

Eight ways to clear duplicate and similar photos from an iPhone or iPad, starting with the free one Apple already gives you. Full disclosure: we make PhotoMuse, one of the apps below. So every fact here comes from each app's own App Store page, checked on 20 October 2026, and we say where another app is the better pick.

Our longer comparison →

The quick answer.

  • Only exact copies, free: Apple Photos → Albums → Utilities → Duplicates.
  • Similar shots too, with the biggest free allowance: Clever Cleaner.
  • Similar shots, a pay-once option and nothing collected: PhotoMuse (ours).
  • Free and nothing collected: Remo Duplicate Photos Remover.
  • Sorting a camera roll by hand: Slidebox or Swipewipe.
Whatever you pick: deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space. Empty that album, or your storage will not change.

1. Apple Photos — the Duplicates album

Best for: exact copies, at no cost.

Before installing anything: open Photos → Albums → Utilities → Duplicates. Since iOS 16 it finds exact copies and merges them. It does not look for similar shots — bursts, or five tries at the same photo — which is the gap every app below fills.

  • Price: free
  • Privacy: Apple's own app
  • Works on iPhone and iPad

Fair questions.

Does the iPhone have a built-in duplicate finder?

Yes. Since iOS 16 the Photos app has a Duplicates album under Albums, then Utilities. It finds exact copies and lets you merge them, for free. It does not find similar shots such as bursts or several tries at the same photo, which is what the apps on this page add.

I deleted thousands of photos and my storage did not change. Why?

Deleting a photo in any app moves it to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where it stays for 30 days and still takes up room. Your storage does not change until that album is emptied: open Photos, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted, choose Select and Delete All.

What is the best free app to delete duplicate photos on iPhone?

Start with Apple's own Duplicates album, which is free. For similar shots as well, Clever Cleaner had the most generous free allowance when we checked, and Remo Duplicate Photos Remover lists no in-app purchases on its App Store page.

Do duplicate photo cleaner apps track you?

Check the App Privacy section of each App Store page. On 20 October 2026, Cleanup, Swipewipe, Clever Cleaner and CleanMy Phone declared Data Used to Track You; Slidebox declared Data Not Linked to You; Remo and PhotoMuse declared Data Not Collected.
