Privacy for Lustless.

The short version: we collect nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.

Last updated 11 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing.

Lustless has no account, no sign-in and no analytics. We do not collect your name, your email address, your location, an advertising identifier, or any record of how you use the app. There is no server involved in using the app at all, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

We designed it that way on purpose. What this app records — the reasons you wrote down, the days you counted, the moments you logged — is exactly the kind of thing that should never sit on someone else's computer. We could not identify you from anything we hold, because we hold nothing.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored in the app's own space on your iPhone or iPad: your reasons, your daily check-ins, each urge or relapse you log, the list of websites you chose to block, and the apps you chose to shield. It is never uploaded, and we never see it.

There is currently no cloud sync or backup of any kind. If you delete the app, all of it is deleted with it. If a future version ever offers a backup, it will be optional, off by default, and described on this page first.

Screen Time and the Shield

The Shield asks for Screen Time permission. iOS does the actual blocking: it switches on Apple's own adult-content filter, adds any websites you typed in, and shields the apps you picked in Apple's own picker.

Two things worth knowing about how Apple built this. When you choose apps or categories, iOS hands the app sealed references it can block but cannot read — so Lustless never learns which apps you selected, and could not tell us even if it wanted to. And the permission is yours: you can take it back at any time in Settings under Screen Time, and you can switch the Shield off inside the app at any time, whether or not you have a subscription.

We use this permission for one purpose only: to block what you asked us to block. It is not used for anything else, and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Lustless Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store: we never see your card, your Apple ID, or your billing details. The app only learns whether a subscription is currently active, which it reads from Apple on your device. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

When the app uses the internet

The app contacts Apple's App Store services to show subscription prices and to complete or restore a purchase. Apart from that, and any link you deliberately tap (this page, or Apple's terms), Lustless does not contact the internet. Counting, logging, the panic button and the Shield all work with no connection at all.

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics library, no crash reporter and no social media kit. Nothing in it reports to anyone. We do not sell or share data, because there is none to sell or share.

Children

Lustless is intended for adults and is rated accordingly on the App Store. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rules such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to see, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show you or delete. Everything the app records is already in your hands: it is on your device, and you can delete it there at any time — deleting the app removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. If a change ever meant the app started collecting something, we would say so plainly rather than quietly rewording a paragraph.
