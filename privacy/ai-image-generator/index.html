Privacy for AI Image Generator Anywhere.

The short version: we collect nothing.

Last updated 28 August 2026

What we collect

Nothing.

AI Image Generator Anywhere has no account, no sign-in and no analytics. We do not collect your name, your email address, your location, an advertising identifier, or any record of how you use the app. There is no server holding your data, because there is no server involved in using the app at all.

We could not identify you or your device from anything we hold, because we hold nothing.

Your images and prompts

Images are created entirely on your iPhone or iPad, by a model stored on the device. What you type, what you say and what you make stay on that device.

Images you create are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting them in the app deletes them; deleting the app removes them all. They are never uploaded, and we never see them.

Photos, camera and microphone

  • Photos. If you choose a photo to edit, the app reads that one photo. It is used on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Camera. If you take a photo in the app, it goes straight into the app on your device.
  • Saving to Photos. When you save an image, the app writes it to your photo library. That is the only thing it writes there.
  • Microphone. If you speak a prompt, speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app requires on-device recognition and will not offer dictation where a device or language cannot do it locally, so your voice is not sent anywhere.

Each of these asks your permission first, and each keeps working without it — you simply lose that one feature.

The one time the app uses the internet

The image model is too large to ship inside the app, so it is downloaded once, from our file host at models.novadevcodestudio.com, and then kept on your device. Generating images afterwards needs no connection at all.

We would rather be precise than reassuring here: that download is an ordinary web request, so the host — Cloudflare, acting for us — sees the IP address that asked and which files were sent, the same as any download from any website. We do not link that to a person, we do not build a profile from it, and nothing about you, your prompts or your images is included in the request.

Apart from that download, and any link you deliberately tap, the app does not contact the internet.

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics library, no crash reporter and no social media kit. Nothing in it reports to anyone. We do not sell or share data, because there is none to sell or share.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Images are generated by a model on the device and are not moderated by us, so we suggest adult supervision for younger users, as you would with any creative tool.

Your rights

Rules such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to see, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to show you or delete. Everything the app creates is already in your hands: it is on your device, and you can delete it there at any time.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. If a change ever meant the app started collecting something, we would say so plainly rather than quietly rewording a paragraph.
