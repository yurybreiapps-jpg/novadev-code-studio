Before you buy

AI image generators with no subscription: what to check before you pay

Almost every AI image app charges monthly, and there is an honest reason for it: most of them are paying a server every time you press the button. If somebody else's computer draws your picture, somebody has to keep paying for that computer.

← All guides The app

Four things to check before paying anything

  • Does it work in airplane mode? The only test that cannot be faked. Turn off the connection and generate. If it fails, there is a server, and a server means recurring costs that will eventually reach you.
  • Is "unlimited" about quantity or about time? Some apps sell "unlimited" access for a month. Read whether you are buying images or buying days.
  • Does a one-time purchase cover future models? Ask before, not after. An app that charges again for each new model is a subscription with extra steps.
  • Is there a free tier that actually generates? Not a trial that expires — a real number of real images, so you can judge quality before paying.

What one-time pricing costs you

Being fair about the trade: on-device apps ask for a large download, a few gigabytes of storage, and a reasonably recent phone. Subscription apps run on anything with a connection, and their models are usually a generation ahead because they run on hardware nobody could fit in a pocket.

If you generate two images a year, a free cloud tool is the right answer and you should use one. One-time pricing pays off for people who use the thing.

Watch the free built-in options too

Apple's Image Playground is free and built into iOS, and since iOS 27 it produces photorealistic images as well as illustrated ones. Worth knowing that its photorealistic model runs on Apple's servers rather than your phone, so it has usage limits, and more capacity comes with an iCloud+ plan. Free, but metered — and that is the same economics as everything else, just quieter about it.
