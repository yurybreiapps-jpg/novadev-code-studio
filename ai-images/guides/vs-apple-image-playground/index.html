Image Playground and third-party generators: what each is for

Apple's Image Playground is free, built into iOS, and since iOS 27 it makes photorealistic pictures as well as illustrated ones. For a lot of people that is the end of the question, and it should be — start there before paying anyone for anything.

What Image Playground is very good at

It is already installed. It is free. It is woven into Messages and Notes, so making a picture and sending it is one gesture. Genmoji is genuinely delightful and nothing else does it. The illustrated styles are polished and consistent, and the whole thing is designed so you cannot really get a bad result.

What it does not do

  • It needs a recent device. An A17 Pro chip or newer on iPhone, or M-series on iPad. An iPhone 14 cannot run it at all.
  • Its photorealistic model runs on Apple's servers. That means a connection is required and there are usage limits, with more capacity attached to iCloud+ plans.
  • It is deliberately narrow. Apple has drawn firm lines about what it will draw, which is a reasonable choice for something shipped to everybody, and a limitation if you are outside those lines.
  • It does not edit your existing photos in the way a general image model can — restyling a picture you took, or enlarging one.

When a third-party on-device app makes sense

When you want to generate a lot without a counter. When you are frequently offline. When your phone is a couple of years old and Apple's tool is not offered to you. When you want many styles rather than a few. When you want to work on photographs you already have.

When it does not

If you have a current iPhone, a normal connection, and you make a handful of pictures a month, Image Playground is free and will do it. Paying for a third-party app in that situation buys you very little.

They also coexist quite happily

Genmoji in Messages, something else when you want a lot of images or no connection. Nobody has to pick a side — the trades are different enough that plenty of people reasonably use both.

Made on the device, not on a server.

AI Image Generator Anywhere runs the model on your iPhone or iPad. No account, no credits, no connection required.
