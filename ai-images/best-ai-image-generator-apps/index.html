Best AI image generator apps for iPhone

Eight ways to make AI images on an iPhone or iPad, from Apple's free Image Playground to cloud apps and fully offline ones. Full disclosure: we make AI Image Generator Anywhere, one of the apps below. The quick answer.

  • Free, if your iPhone has Apple Intelligence: Image Playground.
  • Private and offline, no subscription: AI Image Generator Anywhere (ours).

Fair questions.

Can I make AI images on iPhone without internet?

Yes, with an app that runs the model on the phone itself. AI Image Generator Anywhere, Draw Things, LocalGen and PhoneDiffusion all describe on-device generation. Cloud apps such as DaVinci, WOMBO Dream and Leonardo need a connection, because the image is made on their servers.

Is there a free AI image generator for iPhone?

Apple's Image Playground is free on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. AI Image Generator Anywhere gives 50 free images, then one purchase that is yours for life. Most cloud apps offer a few free images, then a subscription or tokens.

Are my prompts and photos uploaded?

With a cloud app, yes: that is how the image gets made. With an on-device app, no. Check the App Privacy section on each App Store page: AI Image Generator Anywhere declares Data Not Collected.

Why are on-device image apps so large?

The model itself lives in the app, a few gigabytes, so it can work without a server. AI Image Generator Anywhere is about 3.1 GB and LocalGen about 2.9 GB. Cloud apps are small because the model stays on their servers.
