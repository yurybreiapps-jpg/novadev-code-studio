If you do not have that permission, do not import the photo. This matters most for the edits that change how someone looks — those are exactly the ones a person has a right to be asked about first.

What the app does about it

Two checks run, both on your device:

  • Before it draws, the app reads what you typed and refuses requests it recognises as prohibited.
  • After it draws, and before the picture is shown to you, the app checks the image with a model bundled inside the app. A picture that fails is withheld and drawn again from a new seed rather than shown.

We would rather be precise than reassuring: the second check is a classifier, and a classifier can be wrong about a picture nobody thought to test. Every generated image carries a Report control that reaches a person here. If something comes out wrong, please use it.

What that means is plain: keeping these rules is your responsibility, and the consequences of breaking them, including legal ones, are yours. Apple may also act under its own terms, and we will report unlawful material to the authorities if it is ever brought to us.

Models have hardware requirements, which the App Store listing states and the app checks before it offers a download. The app will not offer you a model your device cannot run.
